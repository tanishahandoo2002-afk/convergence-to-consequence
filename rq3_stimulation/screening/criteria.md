# RQ3 eligibility criteria (plan §8.3 step 2)

A record is eligible only if it meets every inclusion criterion and no exclusion criterion.

Include:
- Adults who are healthy, or have MCI or mild AD by standard criteria (e.g., Petersen or NIA-AA).
- Active tDCS or tACS vs. sham, in randomised designs (parallel or crossover).
- An objective episodic-memory outcome: free or cued recall, recognition, source or associative memory, or the memory score of RAVLT, CVLT or ADAS-Cog word recall.
- Enough data for a standardised mean difference.

Exclude:
- Global scores only (e.g., MMSE alone).
- Working memory outcomes only.
- TMS (or any stimulation other than tDCS/tACS).
- Other clinical groups (e.g., depression, schizophrenia).

At title/abstract stage, a record that may meet the criteria but lacks the detail to decide (for example, outcome measures not named) is "unsure", not "exclude".

Title/abstract stage: abstracts rarely report means, SDs or the randomisation scheme. Never exclude a record for not reporting these; they are checked in the full text. Exclude only when the title or abstract shows a clear exclusion (for example, TMS, no sham condition, working memory only, a non-eligible clinical group, or a review).
