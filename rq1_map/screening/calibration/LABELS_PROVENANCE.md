# Calibration label provenance

- 2026-10-18: 30 records drawn (see `draw_log.txt`).
- The decisions in `labels.csv` began as AI-proposed labels with reasons (`proposed_labels.csv`). They were
  reviewed against the titles and abstracts, confirmed without change, and copied into `labels.csv`.
- So the labels are human-verified but **not blind**: the reviewer saw the AI proposals first. Agreement
  between the screening raters and these labels may therefore be somewhat higher than agreement with
  independent blind labels. Report this when the calibration is described in the registration.
