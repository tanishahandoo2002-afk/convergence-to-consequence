# RQ1 eligibility criteria (plan §8.1)

A record is eligible only if it meets every "Include" criterion and no "Exclude" criterion.

| Include | Exclude |
| --- | --- |
| Whole-brain fMRI or PET | ROI-only analyses |
| Healthy adults (18+) | Patient-only samples, children |
| A contrast for *successful* episodic retrieval: hits > correct rejections (old/new effect); remembered > forgotten; source-correct > source-incorrect; "remember" > "know" | Encoding contrasts; semantic or working memory; retrieval vs. rest only |
| Coordinates in MNI or Talairach | Studies without coordinates |

Accepted contrast types: hits > correct rejections; remembered > forgotten; source-correct > source-incorrect; "remember" > "know".

Patient studies that also report a separate healthy-adult group with an eligible contrast are eligible for that group.
At title/abstract stage, a record that may meet the criteria but lacks the detail to decide (for example, whole-brain analysis or coordinates not mentioned) is "unsure", not "exclude".

Title/abstract stage: abstracts almost never report coordinates, the coordinate space, or whether the analysis was whole-brain, so these three are NOT assessed at this stage; they are checked in the full text. Do not answer "unsure" or "exclude" because of them. Judge only population, imaging method (fMRI or PET) and task/contrast:
- include: healthy adults, fMRI or PET, and an episodic retrieval task where remembered and not-remembered (or old and new) items can be compared;
- exclude: a clear exclusion is stated (patients only, children, encoding only, semantic or working memory only, no imaging, a review or meta-analysis, a methods paper without an episodic retrieval task);
- unsure: population, method or task cannot be judged from the title and abstract.
