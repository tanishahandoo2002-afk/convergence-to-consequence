From Convergence
to Consequence

Does the brain's episodic-retrieval network predict memory for single events, and does reaching it with electrical stimulation change memory? Each test sets a meta-analytic map of the network against a domain-general control network.

Study design

One map, two independent tests

The map was built and its masks were frozen (hashed) before any fMRI trial or stimulation trial was opened. The same frozen masks then go into both tests, so neither test can shape the map it is testing.

RQ1 · Map

Where does activity converge when people remember?

Activation likelihood estimation (ALE) treats each published peak coordinate as a blurred probability and asks where the peaks from different experiments pile up more than chance would allow. The input is fMRI and PET contrasts of successful retrieval (remembered > forgotten, or a close equivalent) from healthy adults.

H1a supported in the main map · H1b not run

RQ2 · Predict

Does the map track memory on single trials?

The Natural Scenes Dataset scanned 8 people at 7 tesla over 30 to 40 sessions each while they viewed natural scenes and pressed "old" or "new" for every image. Images came back after seconds, minutes or months, so every repeat is a real memory test.

H2a, H2b and H2c supported
Step 1Frozen masks to each brainEach mask is mapped from MNI into each participant's 1.8 mm functional space with NSD's own transforms.
Step 2One number per trial per maskSingle-trial betas are z-scored per voxel within the session and then averaged inside the mask.
Step 3Label the trialFrom the responses and the design file: hit, miss, false alarm or correct rejection, plus the delay since the last view.
Step 4Mixed-effects logistic modelsRandom effects for participant and image. Pooled across participants and fitted for each participant separately.
RQ3 · Cause

Does reaching the map with stimulation matter for memory?

For every sham-controlled tDCS or tACS trial of episodic memory, the electrode montage was rebuilt in SimNIBS on a standard head. The mean field strength was measured inside the memory map and inside the control network, and a meta-regression asked whether trials that put more field into the map got larger memory effects.

H3a and H3b not supported · H3c, H3d exploratory, not reliable
Integration

Map against control, in both tests

The specificity question is the same in both parts: does the memory map do better than domain-general task regions? The two answers sit on different scales, so they are shown side by side rather than on one axis.

The map does better than the control network at predicting memory, but not detectably better as a stimulation target. Its signal tracks whether a single image is remembered and separates true from false "old" responses better than the control network. In published tDCS/tACS trials, how much of the field reaches the map does not explain which trials improved memory.

For choosing stimulation targets, the trials give no support for aiming fields at this network rather than at general task regions. That is a lack of evidence, not evidence against it. The montages deliver small, similar mask-averaged fields, many trials are small, and one head model stands in for every participant.

Read with care

Limitations

Screening and extraction relied on AI

Title/abstract screening, full-text proposals and contrast choices came from AI raters and an AI assistant. The author accepted the suggestions as one decision rather than item by item, and there was no second human rater. Agreement between the two AI raters was κ = (RQ1) and (RQ3).

Unchecked BrainMap data

17 of the 57 RQ1 experiments were not checked against their papers. Three of the eight clusters, including the hippocampus, depend on them. The verified-only map is reported alongside the main one.

One task, eight people

NSD is continuous recognition of natural scenes in 8 participants. The map comes from studies of other materials and tasks. The per-participant model p-values run small, and the permutation p-values are the safer read.

Modelled fields are approximations

One young head (and one older head as a check) stands in for every participant. 18 montages could not be modelled because a position or current was missing. Off-head return electrodes were moved onto the head model, and some effects were read from figures.

Not run

H1b (recollection vs familiarity) had too few experiments. The HDF5 partial-read test was skipped, because h5py is not installed.
